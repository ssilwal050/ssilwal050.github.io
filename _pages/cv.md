## Professional Summary

Developed a novel parameter-expanded Gibbs sampling approach for Multinomial Probit models to improve computational efficiency and convergence. Experienced in methodological research, large-scale data analysis, simulation studies, and statistical modeling, with a strong record of interdisciplinary collaboration, scientific communication, teaching, conference presentations, and peer-reviewed research.

## Research Experience

* **Graduate Research Assistant**, Michigan Technological University (MTU), Houghton, MI  
  *August 2021 – July 2026*  
  * **Bayesian modeling for nominal measures with missing data:** Designed and implemented a parameter-expanded (PX) data-augmentation Gibbs sampler for the Multinomial Probit (MNP) model, improving posterior mixing, convergence speed, and estimation accuracy
  * **Model extensions:** Extended the MNP framework to multivariate and longitudinal (panel) data settings; integrated principled missing-data handling directly within the PX-Gibbs sampler
  * Conducted extensive simulation studies and real-data applications to benchmark performance against existing approaches

* **Data Analyst / Statistician**, Solutions Consultant, Kathmandu, Nepal  
  *December 2017 – July 2021*  
  * Led data modeling and analysis for large-scale survey projects funded by NORC, the World Bank, and IRI
  * Designed sampling plans, questionnaires, and statistical reporting frameworks that ensured rigor and produced actionable insights for policy clients
